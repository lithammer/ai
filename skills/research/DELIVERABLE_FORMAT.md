# Research Deliverable Format

## Structure

```md
## Summary
[Direct answer in 3-6 bullets]

## Best sources / candidates
| Source or option | Why it matters | Evidence | Risks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Findings
[Grouped observations with links]

## Evidence ledger
| Source | Type | What was inspected | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Negative evidence
[Best arguments against the recommendation]

## Recommendation
[What to use, avoid, read next, or prototype]

## Open questions
[Only questions that affect the recommendation]
```
