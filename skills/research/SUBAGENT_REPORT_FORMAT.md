# Sub-agent Report Format

## Structure

```md
## Scope
[What was investigated]

## Key findings
[Bullets with source links]

## Evidence
[Specific papers/repos/issues/docs inspected]

## Evidence ledger
| Source | Type | What was inspected | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Risks or uncertainty
[What is unclear, stale, or contested]

## Negative evidence
[Best evidence against the likely conclusion]

## Recommendation
[Implication for the main research question]
```
